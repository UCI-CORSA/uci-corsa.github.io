## Research Topic: Efficient Inference for Embodied AI Agents

We are looking for a motivated student to join the CORSA Lab for a Master's thesis or quarter-long research project. This project will focus on designing smart, lightweight systems to make Embodied AI agents (such as virtual/physical robots powered by Large Language Models) significantly faster and more computationally efficient.

The student will investigate innovative caching mechanisms and memory management strategies. This project offers a fantastic opportunity to work at the intersection of AI systems and robotics, improving reasoning speed while efficiently managing GPU memory and avoiding the heavy computational bottlenecks typical of large-scale AI.

## Preferred Qualifications

- Currently enrolled in a **Master's or Bachelor's** program in Computer Engineering, Computer Science, Electrical Engineering, or a related field.
- Strong programming skills in Python or C/C++.
- Familiarity with PyTorch and basic **Computer Architecture** or **Robotics** or **Machine Learning** fundamentals.
- Prior experience with LLM serving frameworks like vLLM, SGLang is a plus but not strictly required.
- Self-motivated with a strong interest in research and problem-solving.

## Expectations

- **Time Commitment:** Minimum of 10–15 hours per week for quarter-long projects, or 30+ hours per week for a Master's thesis.
- **Meetings:** Attend a twice-a-week 1-on-1 progress meeting and the weekly CORSA lab group meeting.
- **Documentation:** Maintain clear documentation of experiments, code, and findings.

## Expected Outcome

- Potential for co-authorship on a research paper submitted to top-tier EDA/Architecture/ML conferences if the project yields significant novel results. Independent students can also potentially lead the project.
- A completed Master's thesis document or comprehensive final project report.
- Hands-on experience with cutting-edge research in AI edge computing and agentic workflows.
- Open-source contribution to the lab's ongoing software/hardware frameworks.
